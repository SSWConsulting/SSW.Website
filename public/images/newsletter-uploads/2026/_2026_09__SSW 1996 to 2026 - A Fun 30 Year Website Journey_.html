SSW 1996 to 2026 - A Fun 30 Year Website Journey

Last month, I posted about SSW’s homepage receiving a 2026 facelift! Looking back, this website has a long history of over 30 years. I thought it would be fun to share a few of the old homepages.

Let's take a trip back in time! I know, I know, some of them have aged better than others. 😂 😂

The first SSW website was built in a very different world. Visitors were using dial-up connections, often at just 28.8 or 33.6 kbps, on small desktop monitors. Developers had limited fonts, unreliable browser support, and no modern CSS. Images had to be tiny, layouts were built with HTML tables, and every extra kilobyte mattered. Even with those restrictions, this website helped build SSW...

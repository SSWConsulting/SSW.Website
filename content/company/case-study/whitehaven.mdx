content: "Whitehaven Coal’s fleet personnel were manually auditing Positive Radio Communications (Pos-Comms) between vehicles. This process was expensive, time intensive, inconsistent, and error-prone: auditors applied rules differently, sometimes passing or failing calls incorrectly. Missed or incomplete Pos-Comms posed real safety risks, including reduced situational awareness, near-misses, and potential collisions. Fleet personnel needed a tool to help standardise audits, reduce human error, and get actionable insights. \n\nSolution\n\nPhase 1 – AI-Powered Automation \\[HS1.1]\n\nSSW developed a custom AI-driven auditing platform prototype that transcribes, and analyses recorded radio communications from Whitehaven Coal’s mining fleet. Leveraging Azure OpenAI Whisper for transcription and Azure OpenAI GPT models for compliance scoring and pattern recognition for identifying positive communications. \n\nThe platform provides personnel with an AI powered workflow\\[HS2.1] that uploads audio files, runs AI transcriptions, reviews and corrects those outputs, and finally exports a detailed reports. Visual dashboards allow for compliance gaps to be identified more clearly, supporting personnel with greater visibility and consistency within their workflow.  \n\nPhase 2 – Reinforcement Learning & Expansion (Planned)\n\nTo continuously improve accuracy, the system will incorporate reinforcement learning, using corrected AI outputs to fine-tune models over time. Future phases also plan to broaden compatibility with additional radio network formats and mining sites. \n\n![A diagram showing future state system architecture.](/images/case-studies/whitehaven-figure-1.png \"Figure: Architecture Diagram\")\n\n&#x20;Figure: Architecture Diagram \n\nExecution & Iteration \n\nEarly in development, SSW pivoted from direct database access to a unified pipeline for audio analysis due to inconsistent formats. Iterative design allowed rapid feedback from Whitehaven Coal, accommodating evolving compliance rules without disrupting progress. Ongoing testing and iterative refinement have steadily improved transcription accuracy and reliability. While still in the prototyping stage, the system continues to improve with each development cycle.  \n\nResults \n\n•\tFaster, more reliable auditing: Tasks that previously took hours are now completed much faster.\n\n•\tConsistent, objective compliance: AI-based evaluation removes assists staff in making audits more reliable.\n\n•\tActionable insights: Dashboards highlight gaps and patterns, so teams can act on safety issues early. \n\n•\tStakeholder empowerment: Personnel can manage the full workflow independently through the portal.\n\n•\tFoundation for future innovation: The platform is scalable for additional sites and radio systems, positioning Whitehaven Coal to lead in the mining communication safety. \n"

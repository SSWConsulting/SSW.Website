### Google Dialogflow with Google Assistant

Dialogflow is Google's human-computer interaction development tool. It focuses on natural language processing and is widely used - Google Assistant is one of the best known examples.

### Microsoft Bot Framework with Cortana

The Microsoft Bot Framework lets developers build, connect, deploy and manage intelligent bots that interact naturally with users on websites, applications, Cortana, Microsoft Teams, Skype, Slack, Facebook Messenger and more. Cortana is focused on business scenarios.

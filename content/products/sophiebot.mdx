## What bots do you need?

* In **finance**, bots can manage giant financial database systems
* In **tourism**, bots can make travel plans and find ideal transportation
* In **manufacturing**, bots can help with production line management and quality control
* And many more industries can benefit from bots

## The benefits of using bots

### Answer questions quickly

With natural language processing, bots understand written and verbal requests wherever you work - SophieBot runs in Microsoft Teams, and the same framework reaches Slack, Google Assistant or your own website. You can ask for help wherever and whenever you want - they're always ready.

### A warm welcome at reception

Instead of leaving clients waiting for someone to respond, a bot with a screen helps straight away. Bots augment your reception and answer the questions customers ask most. Your clients will love it.

### Efficiency, automated

Automation is one of the biggest factors in work efficiency. Bots take on the mundane tasks and enter the data for you.
